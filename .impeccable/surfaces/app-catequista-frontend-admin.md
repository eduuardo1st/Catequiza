# Surface Brief — Catequiza. (Paróquia Nossa Senhora do Carmo)

## Scope and Visitor Mode
- **Surface:** `app-catequista/frontend-admin` (Painel Administrativo da Secretaria e Catequistas) and `app-catequizando/frontend-aluno` (Portal do Catequizando e Famílias).
- **Mode:** Operate (daily pastoral management, live classroom PIN attendance, student enrollment, waiting list triage).
- **Audience & Scene:** Parish secretaries working on desktop screens in bright office light and volunteer catechists opening live PIN attendance on tablets/laptops in classrooms; parents and youth entering PINs on smartphones.

## Confirmed User Decisions
- **Theme polarity & shell:** Warm ivory parchment canvas (`#FAF6EE`) with crisp white surfaces (`#FFFFFF`), anchored by a Deep Carmelite Habit Brown sidebar (`#3B2112`) and Heraldic Gold (`#AF915D`) accents — directly matching Paróquia Nossa Senhora do Carmo's official communication art.
- **Scope:** Both `frontend-admin` and `frontend-aluno`.
- **Brand lockup:** Official Paróquia Nossa Senhora do Carmo coat of arms + `Catequiza.` wordmark with `Paróquia N. Sra. do Carmo` subtitle (Arquidiocese de Brasília — Taguatinga Sul).

## Direction contract
- **THESIS:** Replace the generic dark-blue developer dashboard with an authentic Carmelite parish workspace — bright warm parchment clarity for daily administrative work anchored by the deep brown habit and heraldic gold of Paróquia Nossa Senhora do Carmo.
- **OWN-WORLD:** `#FAF6EE` parchment ground, `#FFFFFF` elevated sheets with `#E5DAC8` sand borders, `#3B2112` Carmelite brown navigation rail and primary actions (`#4E2D1A`), `#AF915D` heraldic gold emblem accents (`#75592C` WCAG AA text on light), `Marcellus` institutional headings paired with `Source Sans 3` UI workhorse typography and `JetBrains Mono` for PINs/CPFs.
- **STORY:** Catechists and secretaries immediately recognize their own parish's trusted visual voice, locate classes, attendance, enrollments, live PINs, and waiting list actions without friction, and families confirm attendance in under 10 seconds.
- **FIRST VIEWPORT:** Fixed 256px Deep Carmelite Brown sidebar with the Parish Coat of Arms seal, `Catequiza.` + `Paróquia N. Sra. do Carmo` lockup, and 5 clear navigation modules; light top header showing `Arquidiocese de Brasília • Paróquia Nossa Senhora do Carmo` and user menu; warm parchment canvas displaying the active module's header, primary action button in Carmelite brown, clean filter bar, and crisp white data cards/tables.
- **FORM:** Brief-pinned Carmelite Parish Identity (Paróquia Nossa Senhora do Carmo), position 1 (brief-pinned); seed: none (unavailable).
